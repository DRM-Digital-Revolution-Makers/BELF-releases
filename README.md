## About BELF

BELF combines workforce management, employee activity monitoring, and productivity analytics in a flexible modular system.

Organizations can select the modules they need based on their business requirements.

### Key Capabilities

- **Workforce Management** — Track working hours, shifts, breaks, attendance, overtime, and employee schedules.
- **Employee Monitoring** — Monitor workplace computer activity and provide visibility into work processes.
- **Project Time Tracking** — Track time spent on projects, applications, and working files.
- **AutoCAD Integration** — Track activity associated with AutoCAD drawings and DWG, DXF, and DWT files.
- **Productivity Analytics** — Analyze working patterns and identify opportunities to improve operational efficiency.
- **Centralized Administration** — Manage employees, devices, permissions, and organizational settings.
- **Modular Architecture** — Choose the functionality that fits your organization's needs.
